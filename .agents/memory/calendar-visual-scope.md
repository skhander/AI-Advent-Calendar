name: Calendar visual scope
description: Preserve different visual treatments for the calendar home and individual concept lessons.
---

Keep the holiday-themed illustrated house on the homepage, while individual AI concept lessons use explanatory diagrams beside the plain-English concept text, without decorative seasonal illustrations.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected a site-wide removal of holiday artwork: they wanted the previous homepage restored but the diagram-first changes to the individual concept pages retained.

**How to apply:** Scope future visual simplifications or "no themed illustrations" requests to lesson pages unless the user explicitly asks to change the homepage again. Do not replace the home house with a concept-card grid as part of a lesson redesign.

Avoid decorative, poetic eyebrow lines above concept titles; keep descriptive subtitles and numbered section labels instead.

**Why:** The user explicitly called those title eyebrows corny and asked for them to be removed across the concept pages.

**How to apply:** When adding or revising lessons, lead with the concept title and useful explanation rather than an editorial teaser above the title.

For typography, use large editorial serif for the home headline and concept names, small functional sans for navigation and metadata, and serif italic sparingly for warm supporting copy.

**Why:** The user asked for clearer contrast between these three levels and asked to remove the homepage's explanatory "Open a door" callout rather than add more instruction.

**How to apply:** Keep the illustrated doors as the homepage's primary interaction; avoid reinstating a competing CTA, explainer, or redundant italic tagline beside or above the house. Keep the main content high enough to appear in the first viewport.
